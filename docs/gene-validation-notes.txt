Presenter notes (intentionally NOT on the slide, at the presenter's request):
The selected results are exploratory. There are 5 OPA1 and 6 DNM1L KO wells,
and 101 / 125 negative-control wells on the corresponding plates. Controls
include no-guide and non-targeting reagents. Many features, particularly
neighboring radial bins, are correlated; 15 hits are not 15 independent
mechanisms. Eight hits also pass the Welch unequal-variance sensitivity test.
The two displayed control-comparison features pass both tests. In both, every
KO well has the indicated direction relative to its own plate control median.
Controls between KO averages does not imply nonoverlapping distributions.
Skeleton trunks are a measured image descriptor, not a direct count of
mitochondria or a direct measurement of fusion/fission rates.

The scatter uses Cohen's d for all 3,651 original named features relative to
each gene's own plate-matched controls. 3,154 (86.4%) move in the same direction.
Orange identifies opposite-sign d and BH q<0.05 in both KOs, calculated using
pooled-variance t tests and BH across features separately for each KO. This
is our correction, not the published JUMP_rr q values. The Pearson correlation
of these effect vectors is +0.814. This is a different representation and
metric from the corrected-profile cosine, which is -0.00714 (not global
anticorrelation). 1,002 features have same-direction significant effects.

The right plots use normalized source feature values, BEFORE our control
subtraction, to avoid making the midpoint result a consequence of centering.
Negative values are possible because these are normalized profiles, not raw
counts or raw intensity. Gray control groups remain separated by the KO's
plates. Both control means lie between the KO means for all 15 selected hits.
